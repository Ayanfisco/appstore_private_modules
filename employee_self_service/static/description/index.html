Empower Your Distributed Workforce with Seamless HR Access

Managing internal communications, data gathering, and operational data entry can slow down administrative teams. The Employee Self-Service Portal solves this overhead by extending secure, role-filtered HR visibility straight to the out-of-the-box Odoo web portal layer. Employees can safely track and submit routine records on their own, seamlessly linking frontend activities directly to your backend workflows.

How It Works

Up and running in three simple steps

1

Install & Configure

Initialize your workspace — lightweight and streamlined

Deploy directly within your Odoo platform. The application relies cleanly on native components including hr, hr_holidays, hr_expense, and portal. Zero third-party core overhead.

2

Activate Employees

Quick user onboarding from standard views

Open an active employee card and hit the "Create Portal User" button. The setup routine provisions a clean login link, hooks up operational parameters to the employee record, and sends access guidance instantly.

Tip: You can manage and activate records en masse directly inside the standard multi-record employee tree view via your typical Action drops.
3

Employees Are Live

Staff log in and navigate frontend tools instantly

Onboarded staff step through the targeted login route, establish safe passkeys, and open their responsive self-service profile dashboard. They can track allocations, file receipts, update personal data fields, and read company paperwork on their own timeline.

Installation & Setup

Quick Installation

  1. Download the archive from the Odoo App Store
  2. Extract and place the files inside your targeted Odoo 17 custom addons path
  3. Navigate to the native Apps application within Odoo backend settings
  4. Activate developer options and trigger "Update Apps List"
  5. Search for "Employee Self-Service Portal" and click "Install"

Activating Employees

  1. Navigate to Employees and choose an target employee record profile
  2. Execute the "Create Portal User" button action block
  3. The target system automatically handles notifications and outputs a login email copy
  4. Personnel complete password configuration changes to gain backend portal features safely
