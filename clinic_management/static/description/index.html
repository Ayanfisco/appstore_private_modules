Online Appointment Booking

Patient Management

Complete patient profiles: demographics, blood group, allergies, chronic conditions, insurance, emergency contacts, and medical history.

🩺

Doctor & Department Mgmt

Doctor profiles with specializations, qualifications, availability schedules, and per-doctor consultation fee configuration.

Full clinical notes (complaint, examination, ICD-10 diagnosis, treatment plan) linked to appointments. Generate printable PDF prescriptions.

Auto-generate invoices from consultations and lab requests. Integrates with Odoo Accounting. Track payment state per appointment.

Prescription-based dispensing with Odoo Inventory stock deduction. Track medicine batches and expiry dates.
