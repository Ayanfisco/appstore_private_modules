Every vendor, customer and employee piles into one undifferentiated list. Finding the right person means filtering, guessing and clicking - every single time.

One giant list

Wrong contacts in Sales

Duplicate records

Wasted time daily

Your team filters the same views repeatedly just to find the right contact. It adds up fast.

The Solution

Every contact in exactly the right place

Smart Contacts replaces the default Contacts app with a clean, role-based system that your whole team will enjoy using from day one.

Smart Contacts Menu Structure

Role-Based Menus

Dedicated menu items for Customers, Vendors, Employees and Business Partners. Land exactly where you need to be - every time.

Role Toggle Bar

Every contact form shows toggle switches for all four roles. One contact can be a Customer AND Vendor simultaneously - no duplication needed.

Context-Aware Filtering

Sales shows only customers. Purchase shows only vendors. The right contacts appear in the right places - automatically, always.

Replaces, Not Adds

Cleanly hides the default Contacts app on install. No duplicate icons on your home screen - just one organised system from day one.

Multi-Role Support

A supplier who also buys from you? Enable Customer + Vendor toggles. One clean record, both relationships fully tracked and filtered correctly.

Works With Existing Data

Installs on top of your existing contacts without deleting anything. All your data stays intact - just better organised going forward.
