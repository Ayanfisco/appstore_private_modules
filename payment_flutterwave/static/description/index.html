💳 Flutterwave Payment Gateway

Accept Payments Across Africa and Globally - Seamlessly Integrated with Odoo 18

✓ Odoo 18 Compatible ✓ Multi-Currency ✓ Real-Time Webhooks ✓ Production Ready

🌍 Transform Your Payment Experience

Flutterwave Payment Gateway is Africa's leading payment solution, trusted by over 1 million businesses worldwide. This module brings Flutterwave's powerful payment infrastructure directly into your Odoo system, enabling you to accept payments from customers across Africa and globally with ease.

Built specifically for Odoo 18, our module offers seamless integration with your e-commerce store, invoicing system, and subscription management. Accept payments via cards, bank transfers, USSD, mobile money, and more - all from one unified platform.

✨ Powerful Features

💳

Multiple Payment Methods

Accept cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), bank transfers, USSD, mobile money (MTN, Vodafone, Airtel, Mpesa), and more. Give customers the flexibility they need.
🌍

Multi-Currency Support

Process payments in 20+ currencies including NGN, USD, EUR, GBP, GHS, KES, UGX, ZAR, and more. Automatic currency conversion handled seamlessly.
⚡

Real-Time Webhooks

Instant payment notifications ensure your orders are confirmed immediately. Webhooks provide real-time status updates for seamless order processing.
🔒

Secure & Compliant

PCI DSS compliant with 3D Secure authentication, encrypted API keys, fraud detection, and secure webhook validation. Your customers' data is always protected.
💰

Split Payments

Configure split payment rules to automatically distribute funds to multiple accounts. Perfect for marketplaces and commission-based businesses.
🔄

Refund Management

Process full refunds directly from Odoo. Track refund status and maintain complete transaction history for accounting purposes.
🧪

Test Mode

Test your integration thoroughly with Flutterwave's sandbox environment. Switch to live mode when ready with a simple toggle.
📱

Mobile Optimized

Fully responsive payment forms work perfectly on all devices. Your customers enjoy a smooth checkout experience on mobile, tablet, or desktop.
🏢

Multi-Company

Manage multiple Flutterwave accounts for different companies in your Odoo database. Perfect for holding companies and franchises.
📊

Transaction Logging

Complete audit trail of all transactions with Flutterwave references, payment methods, and status history for compliance and accounting.
✅

Payment Verification

Manual verification button to check payment status directly with Flutterwave API. Resolve any payment discrepancies quickly.
⚙️

Easy Configuration

Simple setup wizard guides you through configuration. Just add your API keys and you're ready to accept payments in minutes.
